Privacy Policy

OmniDub: Lồng tiếng phụ đề AI (OmniDub: AI Subtitle Dubbing)

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Summary

OmniDub reads the subtitles of the video you are watching, translates them, and reads them aloud. You choose one of three voice options, and where your subtitle text goes depends on that choice: (1) your browser's built-in speech (Web Speech), (2) the OmniDub voice service, run by the developer on Cloudflare Workers, which forwards text to Microsoft's text-to-speech service, or (3) a TTS server that you run yourself (on your own computer, or in your own Google Colab session). To use the OmniDub voice service you request an access token through a Telegram bot, so that service stores your Telegram user ID and username. OmniDub has no API-key features and does not call any AI chatbot by itself. We do not sell user data, run advertising or analytics trackers, or use data for any other purpose.

Prominent Disclosure

OmniDub reads subtitle text and playback state from the video page you are watching, in order to translate and dub it. Subtitle text leaves your device only when dubbing is on and you selected an option that needs it: Google Translate for translation, and the OmniDub voice service for voice option (2), as described in Sections 3 and 5. With voice option (3), text goes only to the server address you entered.

You can turn OmniDub off at any time from the toolbar popup or uninstall it.

1. Introduction & Single Purpose

OmniDub: Lồng tiếng phụ đề AI ("OmniDub", "we", "us") is a browser extension with one single purpose: helping users understand foreign-language video by providing AI-assisted subtitle translation and speech dubbing (text-to-speech). This policy describes how user data is collected, handled, stored and shared, in line with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy.

3. Data Handling & Use

  • Translation: subtitle lines can be translated with Google Translate (translate.googleapis.com). Alternatively you can translate in the Subtitle Studio by copying the prompt and pasting the result back yourself, as described above.
  • Voice synthesis, by the option you choose:
Voice optionWhat happens to the text
1. Web Speech (browser built-in)Text is passed to your browser's speech engine. With voices installed on your operating system the text stays on your device; some browser voices (for example Google network voices in Chrome) are processed by the browser vendor's servers. OmniDub does not receive it.
2. OmniDub voice service (Microsoft TTS through a Cloudflare Worker)The extension sends the text line (up to 500 characters), the voice name and your access token to a Cloudflare Worker run by the developer. The Worker verifies the token, applies a per-user rate limit, requests the audio from Microsoft's text-to-speech service, and returns it. The Worker does not save the text or the audio.
3. Server TTS (VieNeu on your own computer, or VieNeu, Piper or OmniVoice in a Colab notebook you run)The extension sends the text line and voice choice to the server address you entered (for example http://127.0.0.1 or a temporary trycloudflare.com link created by your own Colab session). These servers are run by you, not by OmniDub, and OmniDub never receives this text or audio. In Colab the text is processed in your Colab session under your Google account, and passes through a Cloudflare quick tunnel. Anyone who knows your tunnel link can use your server, so keep it private.
  • Access control and abuse prevention (voice option 2): the Telegram ID is used to confirm group membership, issue a token, apply rate limits and block abusive accounts. A sampled request counter per user is kept for capacity planning and abuse detection.
  • Synchronization: playback state is processed in temporary memory to schedule audio in time with the video.
  • No secondary use: data is never used for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, or any purpose unrelated to subtitle dubbing.

7. Your Choices

  • Turn OmniDub off from the popup. Choose voice option 1 or 3 so subtitle text is not sent to the OmniDub voice service.
  • Edit or delete your settings, token and saved subtitles in the extension at any time.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all data stored on your device.

8. Access and Deletion Requests

You can ask us to show or delete the data held on the OmniDub voice service (Telegram ID, username, block flag and request counter) by emailing the address below, or by messaging the OmniDub Telegram bot or group with your Telegram ID. We will respond within 30 days. Deleting this data ends your access to the voice service; a record may be kept where needed to prevent abuse or comply with law.

9. Children's Privacy

OmniDub is a general-audience tool and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.
